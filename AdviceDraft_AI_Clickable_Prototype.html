Suitability Letter Draft

Dear Mr & Mrs Smith,

Purpose of this letter

Thank you for meeting with me for your annual review. This letter confirms the matters discussed and sets out my recommendation.

Your objectives

You wish to retire at age 60, maintain flexibility, seek long-term capital growth and support your daughter with a future house deposit.

My recommendation

I recommend investing this year’s ISA allowance into a diversified multi-asset portfolio aligned to your agreed medium risk profile.

Why this is suitable

This recommendation is suitable because it reflects your long-term investment horizon, desire for real growth and confirmed tolerance for investment volatility.

Risks you should consider

Investment values can fall as well as rise. We discussed short-term volatility, inflation risk and retaining suitable cash reserves.

Costs and charges

Platform charge 0.25%, fund OCF 0.35%, ongoing adviser fee 0.75%.

Adviser note: Please review tax wording before issue.

Client Follow-up Email

Subject: Summary of our annual review and next steps

Dear Mr & Mrs Smith,

Thank you for your time today. As discussed, your key priorities remain retirement at 60, maintaining flexibility and helping your daughter with a future house deposit.

I will send the formal recommendation letter and arrange a follow-up call before any implementation takes place.

Kind regards,
Sarah Johnson

Client Relationship Brief — Mr & Mrs Smith

Relationship summary

Mr & Mrs Smith are relationship-led clients who value reassurance, clear explanations and adviser continuity. Their main emotional concern is running out of money in later life, although they also want to support their daughter and leave a legacy for their grandchildren.

Communication preferences

Mr Smith prefers a phone call before complex documents are issued. Mrs Smith likes a written summary of next steps by email.

Key family context

They have a daughter who may need help with a future house deposit. They also have two grandchildren and have raised legacy planning as an important long-term objective.

Adviser handover prompts

  • Reconfirm retirement timing and income needs.
  • Ask whether the daughter house deposit goal remains current.
  • Reconfirm legacy intentions and IHT planning appetite.
